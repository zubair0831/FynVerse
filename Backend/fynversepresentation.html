Feature 2: Portfolio Scoring

Five-Factor Algorithm (0-100 Scale)

Score = 0.25D + 0.20PE + 0.25R + 0.15A + 0.15M
D (25%) - Diversification

Sector HHI, sector count, max sector weight

PE (20%) - P/E Rating

Sector-adjusted valuation with PEG ratios

R (25%) - Risk Management

Beta analysis (target 0.8-1.2), concentration

A (15%) - Allocation Quality

Gini coefficient, position sizing (optimal 8-15 holdings)

M (15%) - Market Cap Mix

Large (60-70%), Mid (15-30%), Small (5-15%)

Output: Letter grades (A-F) with actionable recommendations
